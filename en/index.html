Frequently asked questions

How quickly do you arrive at an address in Sofia?

Our response is round-the-clock (24/7). Our team usually arrives at the given address in Sofia between 20 and 30 minutes after the call.

Will the door be damaged during emergency unlocking?

We work professionally and cleanly. In most cases we unlock without any damage to the door or leaf. In unforeseen cases only the cylinder is replaced.

Are ownership documents required?

Yes. Emergency unlocking of homes or vehicles is performed after verifying the right of access or ownership (notary deed, contract, ID card with address).
